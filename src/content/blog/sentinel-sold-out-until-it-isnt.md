![The Sentinel logo: a stylized S made of sweeping blue-to-teal ribbons around a round, eye-like lens, with small check marks riding along the ribbons](./sentinel-sold-out-until-it-isnt.assets/sentinel-logo.png)

Anyone who has tried to buy a booster box, a limited figure, or a restocked pair of
anything knows the routine. The page says sold out. You refresh it. You refresh it
again tomorrow. The one time it comes back, it's gone again before you see it.
Sentinel exists because refreshing a product page by hand is a job for a computer,
not for a person.

You add a product link once. Sentinel keeps checking it in the background, on a
schedule you choose, and the moment the page changes it tells you: a desktop alert,
a Discord message, or a webhook to anything you like. A drop goes live, a size comes
back, a reservation opens up — you hear about it seconds later, not hours.

It works where the drops actually happen: Walmart, Target, Best Buy, Amazon, Premium
Bandai, Hot Topic, Crunchyroll, Shopify stores, even OpenTable reservations. Each
store is a plugin, and new ones arrive in the app without a reinstall.

It runs on your computer. Sentinel does its checking from your own machine, not from
a server somewhere, and your store logins stay on your machine too.

One honest caveat: this is a public beta. Expect frequent updates (the app installs
them itself), and expect to find the occasional rough edge.

Sentinel runs on **macOS (Apple silicon) and Windows**. Grab the beta at
[sentineldrops.com](https://www.sentineldrops.com).
