The short version

This app is used to help plan Pinterest content campaigns, organize product ideas, and track engagement for your products. To provide that service, it may process information you provide, such as campaign plans, product details, Pinterest account information, and engagement metrics. We do not sell your personal data, and we only share it with service providers that help us operate the app.

01 — Who this covers

The basics

This Privacy Policy explains how the Pinterest Campaign App ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by Off By 1 LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy applies when you use the app to create campaigns, manage product content, or connect your Pinterest account.

02 — What the app does

Planning campaigns and tracking engagement

The app is designed for personal and small-business use to help generate content ideas, organize campaign plans, and track engagement for products. In practice, that may include creating campaign drafts, saving product details, collecting performance data, and presenting engagement metrics such as impressions, clicks, saves, and views.

03 — Information the app handles

What you provide and what the app may access

The app may process information you voluntarily provide, including:

  • product names, descriptions, links, and images;
  • campaign notes, content ideas, posting schedules, and promotional copy;
  • Pinterest account details or permissions you choose to connect to the app;
  • engagement metrics or performance data related to your products or content;
  • contact information you provide if you reach out to us with questions or support requests.

If you connect a Pinterest account, the app may access the information needed to display your account data or performance metrics in accordance with the permissions you grant.

04 — How we use your information

To provide the service

We use the information you provide to operate the app, including to:

  • create and save campaign plans;
  • generate or organize content ideas for your products;
  • display engagement or performance information you choose to track;
  • improve reliability, troubleshoot issues, and respond to support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising or to build profiles for third-party marketing purposes.

05 — What we collect

Data you provide and data needed to run the app

We may collect the information described above directly from you, from your connected accounts, or from the systems needed to operate the app. This can include account access data, campaign content, product information, usage-related information, and support communications. We collect only what is reasonably necessary to provide the service.

06 — Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We may share information only with trusted service providers that help us host, authenticate, store, or support the app, such as cloud hosting, analytics, or account-integration providers. These providers are contractually required to handle your information securely and only for the purposes we authorize.

07 — Data retention and deletion

You control your data

We keep your information for as long as needed to provide the app or as required by law. You can delete campaign content, remove product data, disconnect your account, or request deletion of your information by contacting us. If you revoke access from a connected account, the app may stop receiving new data, although some information may remain in our systems until it is deleted or expires.

08 — Security

Reasonable safeguards

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No system is completely infallible, so while we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

09 — Children

Not directed to children

The app is intended for general use and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate parental consent.

10 — Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and post the revised policy on this page.
