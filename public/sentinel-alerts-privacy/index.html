Privacy Policy

Sentinel Alerts

Provided by Off By 1 LLC  ·  Effective July 24, 2026  ·  Last updated July 24, 2026

The short version

This bot does one thing: it sends you notification direct messages on Discord when Sentinel produces a product ping you have signed up for. To do that, it stores your Discord user ID and your notification preferences, and it records basic delivery information so notifications arrive reliably. It does not read your messages, it does not sell your data, and you can stop notifications and have your data deleted at any time.

01 — Who this covers

The basics

This Privacy Policy explains how the Sentinel Alerts Discord bot ("the bot") handles information. The bot is operated by Off By 1 LLC, a Washington limited liability company ("we," "us," or "our"). This policy applies when you interact with the bot on Discord, including when you subscribe to notifications or receive direct messages from it.

02 — What the bot does

Notification delivery, and nothing else

The bot's sole purpose is to deliver notification direct messages ("DMs") to Discord users based on product pings generated by Sentinel. When Sentinel produces a ping that matches a notification you have opted into, the bot sends you a DM with that notification. The bot does not moderate servers, monitor conversations, or provide any other functionality.

03 — Information the bot handles

The minimum needed to deliver notifications

To provide the service, the bot may process:

  • your Discord user ID and username, so the bot knows where to send your notifications;
  • server (guild) and channel identifiers where the bot has been added, so it can operate where it is installed;
  • your notification preferences and subscriptions, such as which product pings you want to receive;
  • commands or interactions you send directly to the bot, so it can respond to them;
  • delivery metadata, such as whether a notification was sent successfully, used for reliability and troubleshooting;
  • the content of the notifications themselves, which is generated from Sentinel product pings rather than from your Discord activity.

The bot does not read, store, or analyze messages in servers or DMs other than the commands and interactions directed at it. It does not request access to your email address, contacts, or other Discord account details beyond what Discord provides to bots by default.

04 — How we use your information

To deliver your notifications

We use the information described above only to:

  • deliver the notification DMs you have subscribed to;
  • honor your preferences, including unsubscribing you when you ask;
  • keep the service reliable, troubleshoot delivery failures, and respond to support requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, we do not build marketing profiles, and we do not use your data to train machine-learning models.

05 — Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your data

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. Notifications are delivered through Discord, so your use of the bot is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy. Beyond Discord, we may share information only with trusted service providers that help us host and operate the bot, such as cloud hosting providers. These providers are contractually required to handle your information securely and only for the purposes we authorize.

06 — Data retention and deletion

You control your data

We keep your information only for as long as needed to deliver your notifications. If you unsubscribe from all notifications, block the bot, or remove it from your server, we no longer have a reason to keep your data and will delete it within a reasonable period. You can also request deletion of your information at any time by contacting us; we will honor verified requests promptly.

07 — Security

Reasonable safeguards

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information, including limiting the data we collect in the first place. No system is completely infallible, so while we work to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08 — Children

Not directed to children

The bot is intended for general use and is not directed to children under 13. Discord itself requires users to meet a minimum age to hold an account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if we learn that we have, we will delete it.

09 — Changes

Updates to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date above and post the revised policy on this page.
